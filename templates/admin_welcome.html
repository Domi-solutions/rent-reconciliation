Welcome — here is how Domi works

Domi keeps the rent records for {{ property.name if property else 'your property' }}: who lives in each house, what each tenant owes, what they have paid, and the messages between them and you. You do not need to know anything about renting to start. Read this once; it takes three minutes.

Words you will see

UnitOne house or apartment in the building, e.g. "A3".
TenantThe person who rents a unit and pays for it.
LeaseThe signed agreement between the tenant and the landlord: rent, deposit, start date, notice.
Charges (bills)What a tenant must pay each month: rent, service charge (cleaning, security) and water.
{% set d = property.rent_due_day if property else None %}
Due dateThe day rent must be paid{% if d %} — here the {{ d }}{{ 'st' if d in (1, 21, 31) else 'nd' if d in (2, 22) else 'rd' if d in (3, 23) else 'th' }} of each month{% elif d == 0 %} — here the last day of each month{% endif %}.
Arrears / overdueMoney a tenant still owes after the due date.
DepositMoney paid when moving in, kept until they move out, then used for anything owed; the rest is returned.
PaybillThe M-Pesa business number tenants pay rent to. The account number is their house number.
Bank statementThe bank's list of all money received. Domi reads it to find who paid.
MatchingDeciding which tenant a payment belongs to. Most are matched by Domi; some need you.
StatementOne tenant's full list of bills and payments, with what they still owe.

Your month, step by step

  1. Create the month's billsAt the start of each month Domi makes each tenant's rent and service charge bill.
    Generate charges ›
  2. Add waterThe caretaker reads each water meter; the water bill is worked out from the readings.
    Water charges ›
  3. Tenants payThey pay by M-Pesa to the paybill. Payments into the paybill appear by themselves; the rest come with the bank statement.
    M-Pesa ›
  4. Upload the bank statement and match paymentsUpload the bank's PDF. Domi matches what it can; you match the rest to the right tenant.
    Payments ›
  5. Follow up anyone behindSee who owes, how much and for how long. Call or message them.
    Follow up ›
  6. Answer messages and repairsTenants write about payments, repairs and other things. Reply so they get an SMS.
    Tenant inbox ›
  7. Money outRecord what is spent on the building and approve what the caretaker spent.
    Expenses ›
  8. Report to the ownerA monthly report of money in, money out and who owes.
    Reports ›
